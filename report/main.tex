\begin{abstract}
Interactive visualization of non-linear chaotic dynamical systems requires high rendering throughput. We present an interactive fractal exploration suite implementing vectorized complex array operations and smooth potential escape-time coloring. The system renders 512x512 Mandelbrot and Julia sets in sub-90ms latency while supporting classical IFS geometry including Sierpinski carpets, Koch snowflakes, and Barnsley ferns.
\end{abstract}
